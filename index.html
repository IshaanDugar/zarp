two ways to use it. pick either.

with your ai

ask claude or chatgpt

Zarp connects itself. Quit the Claude or ChatGPT app fully (right-click its icon by the clock, then Quit) and open it again. Then ask in plain words:

“Using Zarp, what do my notes say about photosynthesis? Give the file and page.”

You get the answer and its page, using up to 75% fewer tokens with Claude.

Works with the Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps (both free to download). Using claude.ai or chatgpt.com in the browser on Windows? Press Ctrl+Alt+Space in the chat, pick the page, and Zarp pastes it into the message box. You press Send.

on its own, no ai

use the search box

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere on Windows. On a Mac, click the Zarp icon at the top of your screen and choose Search. Type what you remember, in your own words.

Each result shows the file and page with a preview. Press Enter to open it right at that page.

windows

Windows 10 or 11. About 130 MB, and it updates itself.

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter. A blue or black window opens.
  3. Right-click inside it to paste, then press Enter. Zarp installs by itself and asks which folders to read.

This is the official installer for Zarp. It checks the download before installing.

Prefer a normal installer? Download Zarp-Setup.exe and open it from your Downloads folder. Zarp installs and asks which folders to read. Pick them and press Start.

Windows warning?

Zarp is not code-signed yet, so Windows may ask you to confirm. This is common for new apps from small makers.

“Windows protected your PC” (SmartScreen). Click More info, then Run anyway.

Blocked with no “Run anyway”? (Windows 11 Smart App Control). Smart App Control can block apps that are not signed. Open Windows Security, then App & browser control, then Smart App Control settings and choose Off. You can turn it back on later in the same place. Or wait for a signed version of Zarp.

mac

  1. Press copy below.
  2. Press Cmd+Space, type Terminal and press Enter.
  3. Press Cmd+V to paste, then press Enter. Zarp goes into Applications, opens and asks which folders to read.

For Macs with an Apple Silicon chip (M1 or newer). It checks the download first, and there’s no “can’t verify the app” step.

Rather download it yourself?

Download Zarp for Mac (Apple Silicon only: M1 or newer), open the zip and drag Zarp into Applications.

Mac warning?

Zarp is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open a downloaded copy, your Mac may say it can’t check the app for malicious software. This is common for new apps from small makers. The one-line install above skips this step.

macOS 15 or newer. Open the app once, then open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.

Older macOS. Right-click (or Control-click) Zarp in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

Then: pick your folders and press Start. Using the Claude or ChatGPT app? Quit it fully (right-click its icon near the clock, or Cmd+Q on a Mac) and open it again so it sees Zarp. On Claude Code, start a new session.

First thing to try: “Using Zarp, what do my notes say about a topic from your notes? Give the file and page.” Or skip the AI and press Ctrl+Alt+Space (on a Mac: Zarp icon at the top, then Search).

Want your usage data deleted? In Zarp open Settings, then Help, and choose Copy my support ID. Email it to ishaandugar@u.nus.edu or open a GitHub issue with it. Details are in the privacy notice.
